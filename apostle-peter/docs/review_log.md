# Review log

The brief's self-critique loop (section 20): for every important shot, look at the opening frame, the emotional midpoint and the closing frame, at 1920×1080 and at phone width. Score it 1–10 on each category, name the three biggest problems, fix them, re-render. Do not approve a major shot below 8 in an important category.

**Who reviewed.** Every score here comes from Claude looking at rendered frames and contact sheets.
- **Motion:** Claude cannot watch the film play. Motion is judged from frame sequences, and the animatic has been viewed only as stills.
- **Sound:** Claude cannot hear. Every claim about sound comes from measurement: loudness, true peak, voice-over-bed ratio, stem levels and sync offsets.
- **What a human needs to check:** before release, a person should watch and listen to the whole film once and write their notes below. That covers pacing, lip-less narration sync, the music's emotional fit and whether any synthesized sound effect reads as artificial.

## Review 1: the full film (all 66 shots), 26 Sep 2026

### Method

Each shot's opening, midpoint and closing frames were viewed from the rendered film (`.tmp/grids.mjs`, one grid per clip), together with the 1 fps contact sheet. Scores are 1–10.

### Verdict

**Not yet at the brief's bar.** Timing, story order, staging and sound hold together. The images do not yet look like "a high-end animated biblical film":
- They read as painted 3-D figurines in simple sets.
- Most shots are below 8 in cinematic quality and animation pose.
- Under the brief's rule, those shots are **not approved**.

Recorded honestly rather than hidden, with the fix list ranked below. The fixes were not made in this pass because the session's budget was spent on building all 66 shots, the score, the soundscape, the mix and the deliverables.

### The fix list, ranked by how much of the film it improves

1. **Cloth.**
   - The chest seam, the flat mantle slabs and the torn-paper edges are in almost every shot of Peter.
   - Fix it by shading torso and skirt on one normal field, giving the mantle thickness and folds, and damping the paint pass on cloth silhouettes.
2. **Seated and crouching figures** (04.2, 09.7, 10.4): build the seated drape from the thighs instead of boxes.
3. **Crowds from above** (09.5, 10.3): replace the dot texture with instanced tiny figures, or a painted crowd with heads and shoulders.
4. **Roman helmets** (10–11): model the rim, the cheek guards and a plume along the crest.
5. **02.5:** clip the water at every phase of the jump, and soften the arms.
6. **Close faces** (07.5, 11.6): pull back to a lit three-quarter in chiaroscuro, so the planar head is never ECU.
7. **05.5:** restage over Christ's shoulder onto Peter, to keep the bible's rule.
8. **Ground and set props:**
   - pebbles: lower-contrast stones;
   - sails: set or brailed canvas;
   - 09.9's sail;
   - the 11.2 rope.
9. **Empty frames** (09.4, 09.8): people, walls and trees.

### Performance (fixed in this pass)

The plate cache was bounded by count (24) and thrashed on the Via Appia, repainting about 40 plates every frame at 9 s per frame. It is now bounded by bytes, and those frames take 0.25 s. The whole 960×540 animatic now renders in 14.8 min instead of over an hour.

## Review 2: fixes after the first public cut

- **Sound:**
  - *Problem:* the bed breathed with the words.
  - *Fix:* per-word ducking is replaced by one steady level per narration clip (8–11.5 dB down). It glides between clips that are close together and only rises in the real silences. Inside a clip the bed now moves only with the music itself: std-dev 0.9–3.9 dB, from the storm, the Tabor swell and the tolls.
  - *Also:* the thunder in 04.4 is 6 dB lower.
- **02.5:** Peter no longer stands on the water. He is clipped at the waterline in every phase of the jump, and his arms no longer spread into a T-pose.
- **Cloaks:**
  - *Cause of the torn-paper patches:* the mantle was 1–2.5 cm off the tunic, inside the renderer's 3 cm depth tolerance, so the two cloths z-fought.
  - *Fix:* it now hangs more than 3 cm out, and reads as one cloth in most shots.
- **Roman helmets:** duller bronze, cheek guards and a narrow crest, instead of a golden bowl with a red plate.
- **Pebble shore:** lower-contrast stones; no more popcorn.
- **Still open:**
  - the horizontal band across the chest in some close-ups: the torso profile was smoothed, but the band remains, and its cause is not yet found;
  - boxy seated crowds;
  - the confetti crowds from above;
  - close faces;
  - 05.5 (Christ's face in close-up).

## Review 3

- **The band across Peter's chest (03.1–03.3):**
  - *Cause:* "Christ's shadow" falling on him was a multiply-blended rectangle, and its top edge drew a straight line across the shirt and the shore.
  - *Fix:* it now covers the whole frame, so the only edge is the gradient's own soft falloff.
- **Close faces:**
  - 07.5 went from an extreme close-up (6600–7100 px/m) to a head-and-shoulders shot in the dark (2600–2850).
  - 11.6 went from 1700–1850 to 1050–1150.
  - The planar head no longer fills the frame.
- **05.5:** Christ's answer is now a wide frame. His face is no longer in close-up, which keeps the character bible's rule.
- **Crowds from above (09.5, 10.3):** twice as many people on a dark ground, so they read as a packed crowd instead of confetti on sand.
- **05.4:** the confession hand is an open palm, where it read as a thumbs-up.
- **02.3:** the furled sails are removed; they read as umbrellas.
- **Still open:**
  - boxy seated figures (04.2, 09.7, 10.4);
  - flat cloaks in the seated close-ups (08.5);
  - empty frames (09.4, 09.8);
  - the 09.9 sail;
  - the 11.2 rope.

## Review 4

- **09.9:**
  - *Cause of the holes:* the set sail was built twice (a front sheet and a flipped copy) on the same vertices, and the two z-fought into jagged holes.
  - *Fix:* it is now one two-sided sheet, and reads clean against the sky.
- **09.8:** the camera was 50 m out, and Peter was a speck on an empty green plain. It now walks with him, with an olive grove around the road and the lake behind.
- **11.2:** the binding was five loops stacked toward the lens, which read as a spring. It is now a tight wrap.
- **Still open:** boxy seated figures (04.2, 09.7, 10.4), flat cloaks in the seated close-ups, and the empty stage behind the preaching (09.4).
