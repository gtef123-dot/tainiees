## Locations

- **LAKE** — Lake Gennesaret, the Capernaum shore: black basalt shingle, cedar-and-oak fishing boats of the 1st-c. 'Galilee boat' type (8 m, one mast), the basalt village above, Mt Arbel's cliff across the water
- **ROAD** — Galilean hill country: dusty track between terraced wheat and olive groves, low stone field walls, the lake glinting below
- **ROCK** — Caesarea Philippi (Banias): the great rock cliff at the Jordan's source, springs, plane trees, fallen boulders
- **TABOR** — Mount Tabor: the lone dome-shaped mountain above the Jezreel plain, Tabor oaks and dry grass, a rocky crown
- **COURT** — Jerusalem, the high priest's courtyard at night: pale ashlar walls, a stair to an upper gallery, a gate on the street, a charcoal brazier on the paving
- **DAWN** — The same Galilee shore at dawn after the Resurrection: shallows, a small charcoal fire on the pebbles, the boat off shore
- **UPPER** — Jerusalem, an upper room: whitewashed stone, a timber ceiling, clay oil lamps, one open doorway to the street
- **STEPS** — Jerusalem, the Temple's southern steps below the Royal Stoa's colonnade: broad stone stairs, the Huldah gates, a plaza that holds thousands
- **MISSION** — The mission years: coastal roads, a harbour with a small merchant ship, open sea
- **APPIA** — The Via Appia outside Rome: umbrella pines, roadside tombs, the Aqua Claudia's arches striding toward the city
- **ROME** — Nero's Rome: brick-and-timber insulae, terracotta roofs, colonnades, temples on the Capitoline, carts, soldiers, crowds (no Colosseum: it is built after Peter's death)
- **ROOM** — Rome, a low room at the foot of an insula: plastered walls, terracotta oil lamps, a stair to the street door
- **CIRCUS** — The Vatican hill: a stone corridor with light slits, then the Circus of Nero's sand and its Egyptian obelisk under a late sun
- **FEAST** — Timeless: warm painted gold, candle flames, window light of a June morning, parchment grain
